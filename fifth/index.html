Fifth Music Theory

Dinky Beats LLC

Fifth teaches music theory around the circle of fifths. Drill notes, intervals, chords and modes in any key, set practice reminders, and use Explore to see how a key fits together.

Get it

  • App Store (iPhone and iPad)
  • Google Play (Android)

Help

  • Practice reminders don't show up. Allow notifications for Fifth in your phone's settings, then set the reminder time again in the app.
  • A flashcard answer looks wrong. Email us the key and the card's question, and we'll fix it.
