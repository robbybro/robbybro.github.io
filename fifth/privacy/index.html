Fifth Music Theory: Privacy Policy

Dinky Beats LLC · Effective October 30, 2026

Fifth is a music theory flashcard app. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. This page explains the small amount of data involved.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your settings: the key you picked, which flashcard types are on, and your practice reminder time.
  • Practice reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No reminder content is sent anywhere.

We never see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Data shared with Meta (Facebook)

Fifth includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK, which automatically reports basic app events, such as the app being installed and opened, together with device information and an app-specific identifier. We use this only to measure whether our app-install promotions on Facebook and Instagram work. Collection of your device's advertising identifier is turned off, and Fifth does not ask to track you across other companies' apps. Meta handles this data under its own policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone audio.
  • We don't sell data or show ads.
  • We don't run our own servers for the app.

Children

Fifth is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
