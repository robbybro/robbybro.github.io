# Sources for what the page says about the Kickr Shift

Every sentence on the page that names a Kickr Shift adjuster or crank length rests on Wahoo's own
owner's manual. A fact that could not be read there is not claimed on the page.

| | |
|---|---|
| Document | Wahoo KICKR BIKE SHIFT, "Owner's Manual and Assembly Instructions" (PDF, 143 pages, created 2023-05-23) |
| URL | `https://www.wahoofitness.com/media/wysiwyg/instructions/Wahoo_KICKR_BIKE_SHIFT_QSG.pdf` |
| Fetched | 2026-11-20 |

| What the page says | Where the manual says it |
|---|---|
| The adjustments are named Saddle Height, Setback, Reach and Stack Height | Page 7, "KICKR BIKE SHIFT ADJUSTMENT POINTS": "Standover Height Adjustment", "Saddle Height Adjustment", "Setback Adjustment", "Reach Adjustment", "Stack Height Adjustment", "Crank Length Adjustment" |
| The crank has pedal holes for 165, 167.5, 170, 172.5 and 175 millimeters | Page 11, step 8: "This design accommodates 165mm, 167.5mm, 170mm, 172.5mm and 175mm crank lengths by simply moving your pedal to the corresponding hole on the crank arm." |
| The manual gives no units for the adjuster scales | Page 7 shows numbered scales on the setback and reach rails in its pictures; no sentence in the English pages (1 to 24) states what the numbers measure. The page therefore says to measure each change with a tape. |
