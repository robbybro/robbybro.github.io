  • Each row moves the hip joint or wrist on the Kickr Shift to where it sits on the Open MIN.D, measured from the bottom bracket.
  • The spread is the variation between pedal strokes. It does not cover camera placement error.
  • Wahoo names these adjustments Saddle Height, Setback, Reach and Stack Height. Its owner's manual gives no units for the scales, so measure each change with a tape, film again, and replace the Kickr Shift video.
