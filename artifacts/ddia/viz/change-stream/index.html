Change Stream Map

The messaging system's change stream from the owner tier to every derived store. Owners serialize each conversation and assign seq; the Cassandra quorum write and an outbox row land together; a relay publishes to a log keyed by conv_id; five consumer groups follow at their own offsets. Lines light up when something crossed them on the last action.

Chapter 12. Partition = hash(conv_id) mod 3, so A→P0, B→P1, C→P2 and every conversation's events stay in seq order. Consumers commit offsets every two events, which is why a crash mid-batch replays up to two and the idempotency key (conv_id, seq) turns the replay into skipped duplicates.
