Messaging System Map

The messaging system from chapters 2 through 10: two regions, each the home for half the conversations. Sequence numbers come from leased owner processes; the leases come from a Raft lock service; messages live in Cassandra at quorum; membership and inbox state in Postgres. Inject a fault, let time pass, trace a send, and switch between the consistent design as built and an available variant that keeps writing on both sides.

        What the map models. Each region homes half the conversations (hashed by conversation ID, with the home region in the ID). A send goes phone → API tier → the owner process holding the lease for that conversation's range → a Cassandra quorum write in the home region and a Postgres transaction on the home leader, then async fan-out. Owners renew a 10-second lease every 2 seconds with the region's lock service, a five-node Raft cluster spread across three zones; each lease carries an epoch that every write is stamped with and that Cassandra checks. Cassandra and Postgres replicate asynchronously to the other region for reads and disaster recovery.

        The two designs. Consistent is the system as designed: an owner that cannot confirm its lease stops sequencing; a region cut off from a conversation's home can read stale copies but not send; nothing is ever decided twice. Available is the multi-leader variant: each side keeps assigning sequence numbers during a partition and reconciles afterward, which keeps sends flowing and produces the conflicts the chapters spent their time avoiding.
