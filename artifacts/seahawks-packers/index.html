Seahawks × Packers — will they play?

A Seattle fan and a Wisconsin fan share a couch. This page tracks the only ways the two teams can end up on the same field this season, what the rules say about it, and the odds from a simulation of the rest of the year.

The odds right now

A meeting can only happen in the NFC playoffs. Both teams are in the NFC, so the Super Bowl is impossible. The higher seed always hosts: Seattle at Lumen Field (the building that was CenturyLink Field until 2020), Green Bay at Lambeau Field.

Playoff bracket rules

  • Seeds. Division winners 1–4 by win percentage, wild cards 5–7. Ties among division winners for seeding use the wild-card tiebreakers.
  • Wild Card round. Seed 1 rests. 2 hosts 7, 3 hosts 6, 4 hosts 5.
  • Divisional round (re-seeded). Seed 1 hosts the lowest surviving seed; the other two survivors pair up, higher seed hosts.
  • NFC Championship. Higher seed hosts. Super Bowl is neutral, so two NFC teams never meet there.
  • Tiebreakers (division): head-to-head, division record, common games, conference record, strength of victory, strength of schedule, point rankings, net points, coin toss.
  • Tiebreakers (wild card): head-to-head (sweep when 3+), conference record, common games (min 4), strength of victory, strength of schedule, point rankings, net points, coin toss. With 3+ teams tied across divisions, each division's best survives first.
