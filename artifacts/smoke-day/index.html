Steps

    Traeger notes

    Smoke is front-loaded

    Pellet smoke is mild and sticks best while the meat is cold and wet. If your model has Super Smoke, run it for the first 2 hours, then back to plain 250°F.

    Lid stays shut

    Every peek costs 10–15 min of recovery. Open it for the foil boat, the paper wrap, and the pulls. That's it.

    Pellets

    The Home Depot hardwood blend suits both meats. Eight hours at 250°F burns roughly 8–15 lb, so start with a full hopper and glance at it around the wrap.

    If it goes sideways

    Skin won't puff

    Slice the skin layer off, scrape the excess fat, and crisp it alone between two sheet pans at 400°F for 20–30 min. Serve as chicharrón next to the sliced belly.

    Running late

    Bump the Traeger to 275°F. Ribs hold 2+ hr wrapped in a towel-lined cooler, so finishing early is free; finishing late is not.

    Stall at 160–170°F

    Normal on both. The paper wrap and the foil boat are what push through it. Don't chase it with more heat before then.
