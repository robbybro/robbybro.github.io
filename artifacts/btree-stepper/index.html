    Keyboard: ← → step, Shift+← → whole calls, Space play or pause. Click any call in the log to replay it.

    This is the classic B-tree of Bayer and McCreight (1972), where every page holds keys and values. Most databases use the B+ variant, which keeps values only in the leaves and links the leaves together for range scans. Splitting, borrowing and merging work the same way in both.
