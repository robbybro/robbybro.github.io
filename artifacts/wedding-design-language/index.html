Colors

Deep periwinkle, ink, honey, and persimmon are derived in OKLCH from the three anchors: same hue families, lightness set for contrast on butter. Olive on butter is 4.0:1, fine from 18px up. Periwinkle on butter is 1.9:1, so it stays display size or switches to deep periwinkle (5.2:1). Tap a swatch to copy.
