Typography

Fraunces first, then a supporting cast that stays in its lane

Fraunces replaced Rubik Mono One as the title face in the most recent round and now leads everywhere. It is a variable font with four axes we actually use: weight, optical size, softness, and the wonky alternates. Big sizes get opsz 144 and WONK 1 so the soft, leaning forms show; text sizes get opsz auto and WONK 0.

Color

Citrus Grove, with a job for every swatch

Derived from the full 43-pin board: cream grounds, olive and honey as the workhorses, burnt orange and coral as heat, periwinkle as air. Butter and cobalt punch in from the cookbook-cover direction. Lilac and bluebell are the wildflower card stocks. Tap a swatch to copy its hex.

Core
Punch
Card stock
  • Text is ink, olive, or cobalt. Nothing else carries words at body size.
  • Burnt orange is display only. It clears 3:1 on cream, which is enough for 24px and up, not for a sentence.
  • Honey and butter are grounds, not text. Put ink on them.
  • Coral, periwinkle, lilac, bluebell never carry text. They are shapes, florals, sky, and paper.
  • One ink per print run. A stationery stack varies the paper color and keeps the ink fixed, the way the board does it.

Ratios are WCAG contrast against cream #FAF3E3 unless the chip says “ink”, which is ink #3B3722 on that swatch. 4.5:1 passes AA for text, 3:1 passes for large text.

Motifs

Drawn in one ink, on many papers

Everything illustrated is single-color line art in cobalt (or ink on a dark ground), the way the board’s doodle suites work. Photography stays real and warm; illustration stays flat and hand-drawn.

The IslandWood map

An illustrated site map built from the pins we love: lodges, the dining hall, the dock, the suspension bridge. Invitation back, website footer, welcome sign.

Parent Trap split hero

The proposal-shoot photo cut in two, Emily left, sliding back together on scroll into the butter “Let’s get married!” lockup.

Tour de Wedding

Our relationship as a stage route: Mount Lemmon, the Gorge, Rocky Mountain, Lake Michigan, NYC, Bordeaux, Mazama, IslandWood. Bikes, jersey stripes, stage numbers.

Starbursts & wavy edges

Card borders scallop or wave; starbursts flag the date or the RSVP. Both come from the pinned Charline & Ethan save the date.

Tomato-can bud vases

Table florals in labeled tins: poppies and wildflowers in coral, honey, periwinkle. The cookbook doodle language, absorbed.

QR as ornament

Every printed piece carries one QR to the site, drawn in the same cobalt as the doodles so it reads as part of the suite.
